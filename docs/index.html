Ten pages.
Zero subscriptions.

Pages rotate on their own; the remote flips by hand. Pick which ones show, their order and how long each stays from a phone-friendly settings page on the homelab.

01

Clock

Time, weather, air quality, UV, the next 12 hours and the next ISS pass.

02

Homelab

Service tiles, last backup, blocked ads and the DVD ripper's progress.

03

Today

Wikipedia's "on this day" with a picture, plus countdowns.

04

Server

CPU temp, load, memory, disk, uptime and a week of internet speed.

05

Chess

Boards from Gambit, rating charts, recent results, the daily puzzle.

06

Live

The top Lichess TV game in real time, drawn on the TV itself.

07

Sky

A radar of aircraft within 40 nm, routes, and the next launch countdown.

08

Radar

The National Weather Service radar loop, fresh within 30 seconds.

09

Tonight

Which planets are up, where, and when.

⚠

Alerts

Weather alerts as a banner across every page. Red is severe, amber is moderate.

Two TVs, one feed.

Lookout started life as a Roku channel. When the Roku's developer installer refused to start, it got rewritten as a native Android TV app, and kept growing.

ANDROID TV · KOTLIN

tv-lookout

The main one, running on a Sony Google TV. Every page, a live Lichess board drawn locally with smooth slides, an animated weather radar, and a screensaver mode so it comes back on its own.

ROKU · BRIGHTSCRIPT

roku-lookout

The original three pages (clock, homelab, chess) as a Roku channel and screensaver. Roku allows one sideloaded channel per device, so all three ideas live in one.

Install.

The feed server lives in latitude-services. The TV app is a normal Android TV build.

# the TV (Android TV / Google TV)
./gradlew assembleRelease
adb connect <tv-ip>:5555
adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
# optional: Settings → Screensaver → Lookout
Lookout is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Made by Rusty. GitHub ↗
